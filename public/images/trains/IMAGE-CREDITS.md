# Kizuna Rail — Train image credits

These credits cover the ten additional train catalog photographs. Source attribution is based on the contributor’s confirmation that the photographs were downloaded from the listed links. Source-page license information was checked on 24 November 2026; exact pixel-level matches to the originals have not been independently confirmed.

All ten supplied PNG files are 1200 × 675 pixels. Processing: resizing, center-cropping where needed, and conversion to PNG.

The photographic assets retain their individual licenses, not the application software license. Adapted CC BY-SA images are distributed under the same indicated license as their source. No endorsement is implied.
